## COOK COUNTY ASSESSMENT RULES (KEY for AI logic)
1. **10% Assessment Level**: Cook County assesses residential at 10% of market value
   - Formula: Assessed Value / 0.10 = Implied Market Value
   - This is the FIRST calculation to run — compare implied MV to actual market value
2. **Board of Review**: Second level of appeal after Assessor's Office
   - BOR Certified value is the official current assessed value
3. **Township System**: Cook County is divided into townships (Jefferson, Hyde Park, etc.)
   - Each township has its own reassessment schedule (triennial)
4. **Tax Bill Calculation**: Assessed Value × Tax Rate = Annual Tax Bill
   - $398,310 × ~2.0% effective rate = ~$8,029 (matches actual bill)
5. **Delinquency**: 2nd installment 2024 was delinquent — relevant for estate/distressed scenarios
